Observations & Analysis

My Categorization Logic: A skin is marked Revealing if a significant part of the character model is bare for no practical reason (midriff, thighs, arms). My rule of thumb is, "Ignoring the fantasy/sci-fi elements, would a sane person wear this for anything other than a beach party, a music festival, or a club?" If the answer is no, its probably revealing. It would feel a lot fairer if the guys got an equal amount of poolside/summer skins, but as the data clearly shows, its overwhelmingly a female-focused design strategy. The few revealing male skins we get are a drop in the ocean compared to the flood of female ones.

Collab Skin Disclaimer: Im not including skins that are direct cosplays of other characters (like from Cowboy Bebop or One-Punch Man). Those skins are just copying an existing design, not creating a new one for the Overwatch hero. Including them would just pad the numbers, especially the Revealing category since its mostly anime collabs.
